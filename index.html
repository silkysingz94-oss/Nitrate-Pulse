05 | Project description

Nitrate Pulse – The Gambia is a geospatial monitoring dashboard concept designed to explore the spatial and seasonal dimensions of river water-quality pressure.

The application provides an interactive view of river and stream networks, geographical filters, a relative screening index and analytical summaries.

The framework can be extended using laboratory nitrate observations, river discharge measurements, rainfall, land use, agricultural activity and settlement data.

09 | Interpretation

Potential nitrate sources
Agricultural fertiliser, livestock waste, wastewater discharge and other nutrient inputs.

Potential environmental drivers
Rainfall runoff, river discharge, soil conditions, settlement distribution and land use.

Operational nitrate-load method
Nitrate load is estimated from nitrate concentration multiplied by river discharge, with consistent units and time intervals.

Validation
Water samples and laboratory analysis are required to calibrate and validate nitrate concentration estimates.
