01 | Project overview

Nitrate Pulse is a geospatial screening and visualisation dashboard for exploring hydrographic networks and illustrative nitrate-pressure patterns across The Gambia.

The dashboard combines mapped hydrography with a clearly labelled synthetic demonstration index. It is designed to support spatial exploration and future integration of field-based water-quality observations.

10 | Interpretation

The index is a demonstration variable designed to illustrate a potential spatial screening workflow.

Do not interpret the index as nitrate concentration. No laboratory nitrate observations or measured discharge data are connected to this application.

11 | Methodology

Hydrography: River and waterway features are requested from OpenStreetMap through the Overpass API.

Screening locations: Illustrative locations are generated for dashboard demonstration.

Synthetic index: A reproducible demonstration score is assigned to each illustrative location. It does not use measured nitrate values.

Classification: The 0–100 index is grouped into four relative classes: low, moderate, high and very high.

Analytics: Descriptive summaries and charts are calculated from the synthetic demonstration records currently displayed.

12 | Data sources

OpenStreetMap – Hydrographic features Overpass API – Feature retrieval HydroSHEDS – Hydrographic reference data FAO AQUASTAT – Water resources information World Health Organization – Water quality

13 | Limitations and responsible use

This dashboard is a prototype for geospatial screening and demonstration. The synthetic index is not a scientifically validated water-quality model.

It cannot establish nitrate contamination, drinking-water safety, pollution loads, regulatory exceedances or ecological impacts.

Reliable assessment requires laboratory-analysed nitrate samples, sampling dates, coordinates, analytical methods, quality-control records and discharge measurements where load estimation is required.
